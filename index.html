The idea

We took over an old print shop and kept the bones — brick, steel beams, the north-facing light. The taps came later, but the habit of hanging work on the walls never left.

Every few weeks a different local artist takes the room. Sometimes it's photography, sometimes it's paint straight off a warehouse floor. Come for a pint, stay because something on the wall stopped you.
